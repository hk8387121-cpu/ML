# DLI-ML Image Dataset Additions

Additional image and geospatial-image sources for the 33 ML features. Repeated sources are intentional because one dataset can support multiple ML tasks.

## Crop health, disease and pest images

- **#4 Crop Health AI:** [PlantVillage on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mohanty/PlantVillage), [PlantVillage Mendeley release](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/tywbtsjrjv/1), [PlantDoc](https://github.com/pratikkayal/PlantDoc-Dataset), [ICAR Rice and Maize Crop Disease/Insect Dataset](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/datasets/details/crop_disease_and_insect_pest_image_dataset_for_rice_and_maize.html), [Plantix research corpus](https://www.plantix.net/en/plantix-intelligence/research/)
- **#20 Pest Outbreak Spread Prediction:** [Bhuvan Pest/Disease Surveillance](https://bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in/pest/pest.php), [ICAR Crop Disease and Insect Dataset](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/datasets/details/crop_disease_and_insect_pest_image_dataset_for_rice_and_maize.html), [Plantix research corpus](https://www.plantix.net/en/plantix-intelligence/research/)
- **#56 Community Pest and Disease Surveillance:** [PlantVillage](https://github.com/spMohanty/PlantVillage-Dataset), [Bhuvan Pest/Disease Surveillance](https://bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in/pest/pest.php), [ICAR Crop Disease and Insect Dataset](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/datasets/details/crop_disease_and_insect_pest_image_dataset_for_rice_and_maize.html)

## Drone, field and plant-counting images

- **#12 Drone/Video Plant Counting:** [Artemis and Image Safari](https://registry.opendata.aws/artemis-image-safari/), [Indian Biological Images Archive](https://ibdc.dbt.gov.in/ibia/), [Kaggle rice plant-counting search](https://www.kaggle.com/search?q=rice+plant+counting)
- **#16 Yield Forecasting:** [Artemis and Image Safari](https://registry.opendata.aws/artemis-image-safari/)
- **#19 Variety Recommendation:** [Artemis and Image Safari](https://registry.opendata.aws/artemis-image-safari/)

## Weed and seedling images

- **#13 Weed Species Classification:** [Food Crops and Weeds](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/nj4vtk4tt6/1), [GrassClover](https://vision.eng.au.dk/grass-clover-dataset/), [Crop-vs-Weed Discrimination](https://lcas.lincoln.ac.uk/wp/research/data-sets-software/crop-vs-weed-discrimination-dataset/), [WeedNet](https://github.com/inkyusa/weedNet), [Plant Seedlings](https://vision.eng.au.dk/plant-seedlings-dataset/)

## Grain, fruit and quality images

- **#14 Grain Quality Scoring:** [Indian Biological Images Archive](https://ibdc.dbt.gov.in/ibia/studybrowse/), [Kaggle grain-quality image datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/search?q=grain+quality+image+dataset), [Kaggle rice-grain classification datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/search?q=rice+grain+classification)

## Satellite and geospatial imagery

- **#11 Satellite Crop Stress Prediction:** [FAO Earth Observation/ASIS raster products](https://www.fao.org/giews/earthobservation/access.jsp), [Copernicus Data Space](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/), [NASA Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/)
- **#18 Soil Health Modeling:** [Copernicus Data Space](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/), [FAO Earth Observation](https://www.fao.org/giews/earthobservation/access.jsp)
- **#29 Climate Risk Scoring:** [FAO Earth Observation](https://www.fao.org/giews/earthobservation/access.jsp), [Copernicus Data Space](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/)
- **#40 Variable-Rate Application:** [CropScape](https://nassgeodata.gmu.edu/CropScape/), [Copernicus Data Space](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/)

## Multimodal and general vision benchmarks

- **#22 Multimodal Query Understanding:** [MS COCO](https://cocodataset.org/), [Visual Question Answering](https://visualqa.org/), [GQA](https://cs.stanford.edu/people/dorarad/gqa/)
- **#28 Anomaly Detection:** [MVTec AD](https://www.mvtec.com/company/research/datasets/mvtec-ad)
- **#51 Livestock and Dairy Health:** [Indian Biological Images Archive](https://ibdc.dbt.gov.in/ibia/), [Kaggle cattle disease image datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/search?q=cattle+disease+image)

> Check each dataset's license, access requirements and permitted use before downloading or training a model.
